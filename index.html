Common Questions

Straight answers before you pick up the phone. Still unsure? Call (562) 924-2565 anytime.

Do you offer emergency plumbing service?

Yes. We are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. If a pipe bursts or a drain backs up at night, call and we will come out.

What services do you offer?

Leak detection, drain unclogging and cleaning, water heater installation and repair, sewer line work, gas systems, and general plumbing installation and maintenance for residential and commercial customers.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Call us, describe the job, and we will give you a free estimate before any work begins. Our rates are competitive and there are no surprise charges.

Should I wait to fix a leak or clog?

No. Waiting usually turns a small repair into a bigger one. Water damage spreads fast and a slow drain can become a full backup. Call early and the fix stays simple.

What should I do if a pipe bursts?

Shut off the main water valve to your home first, then call us. We are open 24/7 and can handle the repair as soon as we arrive.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. In addition to homes, we handle plumbing and drain work for commercial customers, including installations, maintenance and repairs.
